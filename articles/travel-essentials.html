Checking a bag can mean lost luggage, long waits at the carousel, and extra fees. By mastering the art of carry-on travel, you can save time, money, and stress. Here is your ultimate checklist for packing light without sacrificing comfort on your next trip.

If you aren't using packing cubes yet, they are an absolute game-changer. They compress your clothing, keep your suitcase organized, and make it incredibly easy to find what you need without unpacking everything.
